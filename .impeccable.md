## Design Context

### Users
Potential clients evaluating Abbas for product and software engineering work, often scanning the site quickly on a laptop or phone. Their primary job is to understand what he builds, judge his technical credibility, and find a low-friction way to start a conversation.

### Brand Personality
Precise, capable, and candid. The portfolio should feel authored by an experienced engineer who cares about useful outcomes and visual craft, without sounding like an agency or a generic developer template.

### Aesthetic Direction
An editorial and technical working journal: strong typographic scale, visible structure, useful annotations, and project evidence arranged as an index rather than a card gallery. Support equally considered warm-light and ink-dark themes. Google Sans Code is reserved for technical labels, metadata, and controls.

### Design Principles
- Make the value proposition and contact path clear within the first viewport.
- Show proof through specific work and experience, not invented metrics.
- Use asymmetry, rules, and annotations to create an authored editorial rhythm.
- Keep both themes readable, restrained, and visually coherent.
- Adapt the composition for mobile without removing important content or actions.
